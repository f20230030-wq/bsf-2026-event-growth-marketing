# BSF 2026 — Growth Marketing Campaign

Growth marketing for **BSF 2026 at BITS Pilani Dubai Campus**: planning, executing and optimizing the campaign that drives student awareness and registrations.

> 🚧 **Status:** Live campaign (Sep 2026 – present). Updated weekly.

---

## Goal
Drive student registrations for BSF 2026 by building awareness, creating hype and converting interest into sign-ups before the deadline.

## My Role
Marketing Team Member. I work on:
- Campaign strategy and content pillars
- Content planning and the posting calendar
- Creating and publishing promotional content
- Creator and community outreach
- Running growth experiments to improve reach and conversions
- Tracking what's working and shifting effort toward it

## Growth Funnel
```
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Registration → Attendance
```

| Stage | Tactic |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Teaser reels, reveals, campus posters |
| Interest | Lineup drops, behind-the-scenes, countdowns |
| Consideration | Social proof, past highlights, creator collabs |
| Registration | Deadline pushes, story CTAs, direct links |
| Attendance | Reminders, day-of hype, live stories |

## Content Pillars
| Pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Awareness | What is BSF and why go |
| Hype | Announcements, reveals, countdowns |
| Social Proof | Who's coming, past highlights |
| Urgency | Deadlines, limited spots |
| Conversion | Register-now CTAs |

## Channels
Instagram (reels, posts, stories) · WhatsApp groups · Campus posters · Student creators · Email
